Find the best solar panel angle for any town in KZN.

### 2. 🌞🌞 Solar System Multi-Sun V3.3
What if Earth had 2 or 3 Suns? Real physics simulation.

**Live:** https://mfhmultisuns.streamlit.app
**Also at:** https://mfhbinarystars.streamlit.app

**Physics:** `P_new = sqrt(a³ / (N × M_sun))` — Kepler's 3rd Law for N Suns

| Case | Habitable Zone (HZ) | Earth Year | Result at 1.00 AU |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Suns** | 1.34 - 2.36 AU | 258d (-107d, -29%) | ❌ NOT HABITABLE - move to 1.34 AU |
| **3 Suns** | 1.65 - 2.89 AU | 211d (-154d) | ❌ NOT HABITABLE - move to 1.65 AU |

- Total Light: 2.00x (2 Suns) / 3.00x (3 Suns)
- Total Mass: 2.0 Msun / 3.0 Msun
- See live orbit chart + HZ Inner / Outer lines

## 🎯 What Is MFH About?

**Aim:** Build a sustainable, student-run foundry that transforms real problems facing South African communities into validated mathematical models and deployable software tools.

### Our 4 Focus Areas For 2026
1. **Energy** — Solar optimization for KZN households
2. **Water** — JoJo tank prediction, rainwater harvesting
3. **Agriculture** — Smart irrigation for KZN sugarcane farmers
4. **Health + Education** — Clinic queue times + classroom ventilation

### The Five Development Tracks
1. Energy Track
2. Water Track
3. Agriculture Track
4. Transport Track
5. Community Track
6. *Bonus:* Physics Track — Multi-Sun / Orbits
