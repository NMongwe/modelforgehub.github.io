What Is ModelForge Hub About?

ModelForge Hub (MFH) is about Applied Mathematical Modelling + Software Development for Social Impact.

We don't do theory for theory's sake. We take messy, real-world problems and build them into free web tools for South African communities.

What Are We Specializing In?

Applied Mathematical Modelling + Software Development for Social Impact

ModelForge Hub bridges the gap between theoretical learning in Applied Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science, and the practical application of these disciplines to solve authentic community problems in KwaZulu-Natal.

Founded and led by UNIZULU graduates: Ntusani Hlamulo Mongwe (Applied Mathematics & Physics) and Nontando Tsewu (Applied Mathematics & Computer Science).

Our 4 Focus Areas For 2026

1. Energy

Solar optimization for KZN households. Flagship: KZN Solar Optimizer.

2. Water

JoJo tank prediction to prevent dry days. Rainwater harvesting.

3. Agriculture

Smart irrigation for KZN sugarcane farmers. Soil moisture, yield.

4. Health + Education

Clinic queue times + classroom ventilation safety.


What Are We Offering? Especially For 1st Years

MFH is a 3-year skills journey. Join in 1st year and leave in 3rd year with a portfolio.

For 1st Years, We Offer:

1. Hands-on Experience from Day 1: No experience needed. Learn by building real projects.

2. Mentorship: Work directly with 2nd, 3rd year students and graduates.

3. Real Credit: Your name on shipped projects for CV, LinkedIn, funding.

4. Team Structure: Model | Build | Support | Operations

Time: 3-4 hours per week. We work around lectures.
